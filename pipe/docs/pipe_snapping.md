To freeze and mechanically break your 3D-printed natural rubber pipe at its exact midpoint using liquid nitrogen, follow these precise steps.

Natural rubber has an incredibly low glass transition temperature (Tg) of roughly -70°C (-94°F). Standard freezing will keep it flexible, but immersing it in liquid nitrogen (-196°C / -320°F) brings it well below its brittle point, transforming the elastic rubber into a highly fragile, glass-like state that is ready for a clean snap.

Safety First (Mandatory)
---------------------------

-   Skin & Eye Protection: Wear heavy, insulated cryogenic gloves and a full-face shield. Liquid nitrogen causes rapid, severe frostbite upon skin contact.
-   Ventilation: Perform this procedure only in a well-ventilated space or under a fume hood. Evaporating nitrogen gas displaces oxygen rapidly and can cause asphyxiation.
-   Tool Handling: Use long-handled stainless steel tongs to manipulate the pipe. Never touch the frozen pipe with bare hands.

* * * * *

Step-by-Step Breaking Procedure
-----------------------------------

1\. Pre-Notch the Break Line (Optional but Recommended)
-------------------------------------------------------

Before freezing, use a sharp razor blade to score a shallow, structural ring around the exact middle axis of the pipe where you want the split to occur. This notch acts as a stress concentrator, ensuring the mechanical break occurs cleanly along that line rather than shattering the body.

2\. Cryogenic Submersion
------------------------

1.  Fill a vacuum-insulated dewar or an appropriate cryogenic container with enough liquid nitrogen to fully submerge the midpoint of the pipe.
2.  Using stainless steel tongs, lower the pipe slowly into the liquid nitrogen.
3.  Leave the pipe submerged for 60 to 90 seconds. You will notice intense boiling initially; wait until the boiling slows down, which indicates the rubber has thoroughly chilled to equilibrium temperature.

3\. Mechanical Break Alignment
------------------------------

1.  Act Quickly: Once removed from the liquid nitrogen, the rubber will begin absorbing ambient heat immediately. You have roughly a 5 to 10-second window before it loses its brittleness.
2.  Transfer the frozen pipe immediately into your mechanical bending rig or test machine (such as a 3-point bend fixture or a hydraulic press setup).
3.  Ensure the pre-notched middle line is aligned perfectly with the machine's central bending wedge or pressure anvil.

4\. Load Application
--------------------

1.  Apply a sudden, sharp bending load using your machine.
2.  Because the rubber is completely vitrified (turned to glass), a rapid mechanical impact or sharp bend force will cause a clean, instantaneous cleavage fracture right through the center point without tearing or stretching.
3.  Allow both broken halves to warm back up to room temperature naturally. Once warm, they will completely regain their original soft, flexible natural rubber elastomeric properties.
