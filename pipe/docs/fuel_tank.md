To prevent dangerous, volatile reactions—such as "direct quantum fuel explosions"—during rapid fuel harvesting or high-energy transfers, we can design a Buffer/Reserve Tank System.
By utilizing the mechanical-to-quantum state bridge logic from the signal buffer repository, this system acts as a physical and energetic "airlock." Instead of directly pumping raw, highly reactive energy or propellant straight into your main propulsion engines, the fuel is safely isolated, stabilized, and stored in a secondary reserve. It can then be fed into your primary systems steadily during down-times ("when not harvesting").

## Core Components & Mechanics

* 
* The Quantum Gate (Primary Isolator):
This is the intake valve. Acting similarly to the remote signal buffer, it maintains a strict "state bridge." When open, fuel pours in. The moment a critical threshold or instability is detected, it cleanly snaps shut magnetically and digitally, instantly severing the energetic link so a feedback explosion cannot travel back up the harvesting line.
* The Flux-Dampening Reserve Tank:
A double-walled, magnetically suspended containment cell. The harvested fuel sits here first to bleed off dangerous kinetic energy, quantum entanglement spikes, or thermal pressure. It treats the raw fuel as "volatile data" that needs to sit in a buffer cache before being written to permanent storage.
* The Asynchronous Feed Controller:
An automated interlock system ensuring that the Intake Valve and the Engine Feed Valve can never be open at the same time. This completely eliminates the risk of a "direct explosion" by ensuring the engine is fed strictly from stabilized reserve fuel during quiet, non-harvesting cycles.

## Operational Lifecycle

* 
* Phase 1: Harvesting Mode (Intake Open, Feed Closed)
The vehicle gathers raw cosmic or quantum fuel. The fuel fills the Reserve Tank. The main engine is completely partitioned and safe from any volatile spikes happening at the scoop.
* Phase 2: Stabilization (All Valves Closed)
The signal buffer circuit analyzes the tank's telemetry. It waits for pressure, quantum spin, or molecular agitation to settle into a safe state.
* Phase 3: Cruising/Feeding Mode (Intake Closed, Feed Open)
With harvesting paused, the stable fuel is cleanly drafted from the reserve tank into the propulsion system. Safe, controlled, and predictable thrust is achieved.
